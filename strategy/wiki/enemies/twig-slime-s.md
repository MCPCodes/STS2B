# Twig Slime (S)
Overgrowth small slime, 7-11 HP: hits 4 every turn and does nothing else.

## Facts (game files, v0.107.1)

Where: act 1 Overgrowth only.
- Hallway fight SlimesWeak (weak): 3 enemies. e0 and e2 are 1 Leaf Slime (S) and 1 Twig Slime (S), order 50/50. e1 is Leaf Slime (M) or Twig Slime (M), 50/50. Weak fights come only as hallway fights 1-3 of the act.
- Hallway fight SlimesNormal (normal): 4 enemies. Twig Slime (M) e0, Leaf Slime (M) e1, then Leaf Slime (S) and Twig Slime (S) as e2 and e3, order 50/50.
- Hallway fight SlitheringStranglerNormal (normal), 1 fight in 3: 2 small slimes, then Slithering Strangler last. Each small slime is Leaf or Twig, 50/50, so both can be Twig Slime (S).
- Normal fights come from hallway fight 4 on, each at most once per act.

Companions:
- Leaf Slime (S): 11-15 HP. Alternates 3 damage and 1 Slimed; which comes first is 50/50.
- Leaf Slime (M): 32-35 HP. 2 Slimed on odd enemy turns, 8 damage on even ones.
- Twig Slime (M): 26-28 HP. 1 Slimed on enemy turn 1, 11 damage on turn 2, then 11 damage or 1 Slimed: never Slimed twice in a row, at most 2 hits in a row.
- Slithering Strangler: 53-55 HP. Adds 3 Constrict on odd enemy turns, hits 7 or 12 on even ones.

HP: 7-11 (asc 8+: 8-12).

Starts with: no powers.

Moves:

| Move | Intent shown | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tackle | `attack 4` | 1 hit: 4 (asc 9+: 5). |

Pattern: the same move every enemy turn, from turn 1. No randomness.

Special rules:
- It does not split or spawn anything when hit or killed.
- It never gains Block and applies no debuffs.
- No summon, heal, escape, on-death effect or reaction to hits or cards. The fight ends when every enemy is dead.

Status cards: none. The other slimes add Slimed (cost 1, Exhaust, draws 1 card when played, harmless in hand).

## How to fight it
Derived from the facts above, not from play.
- 4 damage every turn for 7-11 HP: the most damage per HP of the 4 slimes. Kill it first.
- 1 Strike (6) never kills it. 2 Strikes (12) always do.
- Bash (8) kills it at 7-8 HP. 1 Strike into Vulnerable (9) kills it at 9 HP or less.
