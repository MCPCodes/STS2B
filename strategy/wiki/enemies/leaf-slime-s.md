# Leaf Slime (S)
Overgrowth small slime, 11-15 HP: alternates a hit of 3 and adding 1 Slimed to your discard pile; which comes first is 50/50.

## Facts (game files, v0.107.1)

Where: act 1 Overgrowth only.
- Hallway fight SlimesWeak (weak): 3 enemies. e0 and e2 are 1 Leaf Slime (S) and 1 Twig Slime (S), order 50/50. e1 is Leaf Slime (M) or Twig Slime (M), 50/50. Weak fights come only as hallway fights 1-3 of the act.
- Hallway fight SlimesNormal (normal): 4 enemies. Twig Slime (M) e0, Leaf Slime (M) e1, then Leaf Slime (S) and Twig Slime (S) as e2 and e3, order 50/50.
- Hallway fight SlitheringStranglerNormal (normal), 1 fight in 3: 2 small slimes, then Slithering Strangler last. Each small slime is Leaf or Twig, 50/50, so both can be Leaf Slime (S).
- Normal fights come from hallway fight 4 on, each at most once per act.

Companions:
- Twig Slime (S): 7-11 HP. Hits 4 every turn.
- Leaf Slime (M): 32-35 HP. 2 Slimed on odd enemy turns, 8 damage on even ones.
- Twig Slime (M): 26-28 HP. 1 Slimed on enemy turn 1, 11 damage on turn 2, then 11 damage or 1 Slimed: never Slimed twice in a row, at most 2 hits in a row.
- Slithering Strangler: 53-55 HP. Adds 3 Constrict on odd enemy turns, hits 7 or 12 on even ones.

HP: 11-15 (asc 8+: 12-16).

Starts with: no powers.

Moves:

| Move | Intent shown | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tackle | `attack 3` | 1 hit: 3 (asc 9+: 4). |
| Goop | `statuscard: 1` | Adds 1 Slimed to your discard pile. No damage. |

Pattern:
- First move: Tackle or Goop, 50/50.
- After that the 2 strictly alternate.
- The turn 1 intent fixes the whole fight: that move on every odd enemy turn, the other on every even one.

Special rules:
- It does not split or spawn anything when hit or killed.
- It never gains Block and applies no debuffs.
- No summon, heal, escape, on-death effect or reaction to hits or cards. The fight ends when every enemy is dead.

Status card: Slimed. Status, cost 1, Exhaust, cannot be upgraded.
- Playing it draws 1 card. Then it is exhausted.
- Left in hand it does nothing. It is discarded with the hand.
- It goes to your discard pile during the slime's move. It reaches your hand only after the next reshuffle.

## How to fight it
Derived from the facts above, not from play.
- Lowest threat in its fights: 3 damage every 2nd turn. Kill Twig Slime (S) (4 every turn, 7-11 HP) before it.
- 2 Strikes (12) kill it at 11-12 HP. Bash + Strike (8 + 9 = 17) always kills it.
- Slimed costs 1 energy to trade for 1 draw and leaves the deck for the fight. Play it with energy to spare; holding it costs no HP.
