# Ironclad Powers: when to play them
When to play each Ironclad Power in your hand and where it goes in the turn, worked out from the card facts, with how often scripted fights played it and what it was worth.

## How to use this page
- Count the turns left before you play a Power, because it pays over them. Inflame matches the Strike it replaces after 3 later hits, Pyre breaks even after 2 turns, and Demon Form beats 3 Strikes only with 3 or more turns left (at 2 hits a turn: 24 against 18). Short hallway fights: frontload (general.md 8.8).
- Judge it by this turn's danger (general.md 8.8). With no damage coming, or damage already covered by Block you were going to play, set up: the Power costs no HP. On a dangerous turn, block first and play a Power only if it pays back this turn, or the numbers in its entry say the HP is worth it.
- A Power you don't play is discarded with the hand at end of turn. Waiting for a quiet turn means waiting until you draw it again after a reshuffle, so count that delay against the HP it would cost now.
- Sort the Power in hand into one of two kinds. Pays this turn, so it can go on a dangerous turn when the right cards follow: Unmovable before your one Block card (Defend gives 10 instead of 5), Inflame before a multi-hit (3 hits this turn repay it), Juggernaut before 2 or more Block gains, Feel No Pain before 2 or more exhausts, Rupture or Inferno before an HP-loss card, Corruption with 4 Skills, Stampede with an Attack you can't afford, Stone Armor (4 Block this turn against a Defend's 5). Vicious, Dark Embrace and Hellraiser count only when you can still play what they draw. Pays later, so it wants a quiet turn: Demon Form, Aggression, Pyre, Barricade, and any engine with nothing to trigger it this turn. Crimson Mantle pays from next turn and is ahead of a Defend after one more hit of 8 or more, so it can go on a hit turn too.
- Quiet turns are scarce: of the elite and boss turns on which a Power could be played, about 1 in 4 had nothing coming through and two in three had 10 or more. Against bosses (9 to 10 turns) both scripts played Demon Form into a large hit about as often as on a quiet turn (70% and 71% of large turns, 71% and 64% of quiet ones); against elites (about 6 turns) they mostly kept it back (8% and 19% of large turns). With the play-out script it was worth more against bosses (+10.0 HP) than against elites (+4.2). Whether playing it into the hit was the better turn, the measured page doesn't say.
- Nothing on the turn played (facts page): Aggression, Crimson Mantle, Demon Form, Pyre, and Inferno's own HP loss. Barricade first matters at the start of your next turn. A played Power leaves the fight without being exhausted, so it never triggers Feel No Pain, Dark Embrace, Evil Eye or Forgotten Ritual.
- The timing advice is worked out from the card facts (strategy/wiki/cards/ironclad.md) and the boss and enemy pages; none of it has been tested over whole runs yet. Each entry's **Measured** line comes from ironclad-power-timing.md: on what share of quiet (nothing coming through), small-hit (1 to 9) and large-hit (10 or more) turns the play-out script played it when it could, in single act 1 elite and boss fights, and the HP it was worth against one more Strike. A guide, not a rule: the decks weren't built for the card. The small-hit share rests on 26 to 52 turns a Power, most of them against bosses: read it as rough. The one-turn figure in brackets is on that script's own scale (control cards came out 1.1 HP higher with the play-out script), so don't read the gap between the two as what better play adds; only the three cards marked (low) moved clearly beyond the controls. Bosses and elites: the play-out script's worth split by kind of fight, for the six Powers the measured page splits. Each Power is timed differently, so read its own entry.

## Powers

### Strength and attacks

#### Inflame (1 energy; upgraded: 3 Strength instead of 2)
Gain 2 Strength (upgraded 3) at once, for the rest of the fight.
- **Pays:** this turn: +2 on every later hit (+3 on a Vulnerable enemy). 2 hits a turn over 4 turns is 16 (upgraded 24). A Strike in its place deals 6, so Inflame matches it after 3 later hits (upgraded 2) and gains from then on.
- **Play it:** any quiet turn. On a big-hit turn, with energy left after the Block you need: it costs no HP then, so the test is the hits left in the fight (Hold it). 3 or more hits this turn (upgraded 2) repay it at once (Sword Boomerang alone gives +6, a Strike's worth; Twin Strike alone +4).
- **Hold it:** when 3 or fewer hits are left in the fight (upgraded 2 or fewer), or all energy must go to Block.
- **In the turn:** before every Attack; Thrash's growth includes your Strength. Inferno, Juggernaut and Flame Barrier ignore it. With Ruined Helmet (first Strength gain each fight doubled), make the first gain your biggest.
- **Measured:** played on 91% of quiet, 76% of small-hit and 75% of large-hit turns; worth +5.2 ±1.4 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +3.2).

#### Demon Form (3 energy; upgraded: 3 Strength a turn instead of 2)
At the start of each of your turns, gain 2 Strength (upgraded 3). Nothing on the turn it is played.
- **Pays:** from your next turn, +2 a turn: at 2 hits a turn, 4 + 8 + 12 = 24 damage over 3 turns.
- **Play it:** on a quiet or cheap turn: Bygone Effigy turns 1-2, Ceremonial Beast turn 1 and the turn after its stun, Vantom turns 4 and 8, The Kin turns 1 and 4 (13 and 7 damage), Lagavulin Matriarch turns 1-3 unless your damage wakes her early. In a boss fight (9 to 10 turns), take a hit for it when you can spare the HP: unplayed, it is discarded until a reshuffle. A hit that would kill you: block.
- **Hold it:** if the fight ends within 2 more turns: at 2 hits a turn, 3 Strikes deal 18 now, Demon Form 12 by then. Against an elite (about 6 turns), on a big-hit turn: it costs all your Block, unless Bloodletting or Offering pays for some.
- **In the turn:** energy cards first. Before Second Wind, Stoke, Fiend Fire, unupgraded True Grit or Cinder, which can exhaust it from hand. Headbutt can put a discarded one back on the draw pile.
- **Measured:** played on 75% of quiet, 59% of small-hit and 47% of large-hit turns; worth +7.1 ±1.6 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +4.0). Bosses +10.0, elites +4.2. Marked (low): more than the pick-values row says.

#### Rupture (1 energy; upgraded: 2 Strength per HP loss instead of 1)
Each HP loss on your own turn gives 1 Strength (upgraded 2). HP lost on the enemy turn never counts.
- **Pays:** at once, per loss, not per HP: Bloodletting, Offering, Brand, Hemokinesis, Breakthrough, Blood Wall; Inferno and Crimson Mantle every turn. With Inferno out, 2 hits a turn gain 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20 over 4 turns.
- **Play it:** before an HP-loss card this turn. Rupture then Bloodletting is still 1 energy up, so it fits even a big-hit turn. On a quiet turn if HP-loss cards are left to draw.
- **Hold it:** no HP-loss card in the deck. A big hit coming and none in hand: block. Below half HP, spend HP only to win.
- **In the turn:** before every HP-loss card. Brand after it gives 2 Strength (3 if either is upgraded, 4 if both). Hemokinesis and Breakthrough miss their own trigger: play them before other attacks.
- **Measured:** played on 67% of quiet, 53% of small-hit and 38% of large-hit turns; worth -0.1 ±1.4 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -0.3).

#### Cruelty (1 energy; upgraded: x2.0 instead of x1.75)
Your attacks on Vulnerable enemies are multiplied by 1.75 instead of 1.5 (upgraded 2.0).
- **Pays:** at once, on each hit into a Vulnerable enemy: 20 damage a turn there (Strength included) becomes 35 instead of 30 (upgraded 40), +20 over 4 turns (upgraded +40). At that rate it beats a Strike into a Vulnerable enemy (9) after 2 turns.
- **Play it:** when an enemy is Vulnerable or will be this turn, with attacks to follow; on a quiet turn when Vulnerable is up most turns (Break, or 3+ sources).
- **Hold it:** no Vulnerable up and no source in hand: it pays nothing now, so on a big-hit turn, block. Artifact cancels the next Vulnerable.
- **In the turn:** Vulnerable and Cruelty before the attacks; Bash, Uppercut and Thunderclap hit before applying theirs. Every hit of a multi-hit, Dismantle and Bully gains; flat damage does not. Paper Phrog and extra copies add up.
- **Measured:** played on 63% of quiet, 51% of small-hit and 43% of large-hit turns; worth -0.8 ±1.3 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -1.2).

#### Vicious (1 energy; upgraded: draw 2 instead of 1)
Whenever you apply Vulnerable, draw 1 (upgraded 2) for each enemy that receives it.
- **Pays:** on each application after it, this turn included. Thunderclap on 3 enemies draws 3 (6 with Vicious upgraded). Nothing when Artifact cancels it.
- **Play it:** when you apply Vulnerable after it and have energy to play the draw: from 3 energy, Vicious then Tremble leaves 1; Vicious then Bash leaves 0, so only a 0-cost draw is playable. On a quiet turn in a deck that applies Vulnerable most turns.
- **Hold it:** no Vulnerable card in hand and few in the deck. It gives no Block. With 4 more Vulnerable cards in the deck it measured about level with a Strike (synergies page).
- **In the turn:** before Bash, Tremble, Taunt, Uppercut, Dominate and Thunderclap, and all of them before Battle Trance, which blocks later draws.
- **Measured:** played on 51% of quiet, 46% of small-hit and 28% of large-hit turns; worth -2.0 ±1.3 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -2.7).

#### Juggling (1 energy; upgraded: Innate)
When your 3rd Attack play of a turn finishes, add a copy of that Attack to your hand.
- **Pays:** once a turn; this turn only if 3 Attacks fit in the other 2 energy. The copy keeps its upgrade, and is free this turn only if the played card was: a copied Anger is 6 free damage, but three 1-cost Attacks on 3 energy leave nothing for their copy.
- **Play it:** when your turns hold 3+ Attacks and energy for the copy: 0-cost Attacks (Anger, Bully, Spite), free plays, Bloodletting or Offering. Or on a quiet turn.
- **Hold it:** when that is rare in your deck, or on a big-hit turn: it gives no Block.
- **In the turn:** Attacks played before it count: play it before the 3rd, and make the 3rd the one you want twice. Free plays (Havoc, Cascade, Hellraiser) and One-Two Punch repeats count. Stomp as the 3rd costs 1, its copy 0 (inferred).
- **Measured:** played on 54% of quiet, 50% of small-hit and 33% of large-hit turns; worth -0.5 ±1.5 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -0.4).

#### Aggression (1 energy; upgraded: Innate)
At the start of each of your turns, 1 random Attack moves from your discard pile to your hand, upgraded for the fight. Nothing the turn it is played.
- **Pays:** from your next turn, once a turn: 5 drawn cards plus an upgraded Attack (a Strike returns as 9). It gives no energy, so unless the Attack costs 0 it replaces your weakest play.
- **Play it:** on a quiet turn, or with 1 energy spare after the Block you need, when 3+ turns are left (inferred). Upgraded it is in your opening hand: play it turn 1 unless that turn's Block needs all 3 energy.
- **Hold it:** if the fight ends within 2 turns, or the energy must go to Block.
- **In the turn:** before Second Wind, Stoke, Fiend Fire, unupgraded True Grit or Cinder, which can exhaust it from hand; otherwise order does not matter. Not a draw: Hellraiser does not play a returned Strike. Anger copies come back upgraded, still 0 cost.
- **Measured:** played on 81% of quiet, 78% of small-hit and 58% of large-hit turns; worth +5.5 ±1.6 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +2.8). Bosses +7.2, elites +3.7. Marked (low): more than the pick-values row says.

### Block

#### Feel No Pain (1 energy; upgraded: 4 Block per exhaust instead of 3)
Whenever a card of yours is exhausted, gain 3 flat Block.
- **Pays:** on every exhaust after you play it, this turn included. 2 exhausts a turn over 4 turns: 2 x 3 x 4 = 24 Block (upgraded 32).
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, or a dangerous turn with 2 or more exhausts to follow (2 x 3 = 6 beats a Defend's 5). Feel No Pain, then Second Wind on 3 other cards: 15 + 9 = 24 Block for 2 energy.
- **Hold it:** a hit you must block and no exhaust to follow: it blocks 0 now. Late in a fight with few exhausts left to draw.
- **In the turn:** before any exhaust. Second Wind, Fiend Fire, Stoke, Cinder and unupgraded True Grit can exhaust it from your hand. A played Exhaust card (Tremble, Offering) triggers it, and under Corruption every Skill does. With Juggernaut out, each exhaust also deals 6.
- **Measured:** played on 56% of quiet, 54% of small-hit and 37% of large-hit turns; worth -0.5 ±1.4 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -1.5).

#### Stone Armor (1 energy; upgraded: 6 Plating instead of 4)
Gain 4 Plating. At the end of your turn, gain flat Block equal to Plating. Plating drops by 1 at the start of each later turn.
- **Pays:** at the end of this turn, then each turn: 4, 3, 2, 1 = 10 (upgraded 21). Hits do not lower it.
- **Play it:** early, on a turn with a hit coming: the first payment is the biggest. That turn it is nearly a Defend (4 against 5) plus 6 later; upgraded (6), or while you are Frail (Defend 3), it beats one at once.
- **Hold it:** a quiet turn wastes the first payment unless Barricade is out. Against Bygone Effigy it blocks 3 of the Slashes if played on turn 1, all 10 on turn 3. With nothing better for the energy, play it anyway.
- **In the turn:** order does not matter. Its Block lands before end-of-turn Burn and Infection damage, too late for Body Slam that turn. Juggernaut fires on each payment; Unmovable does not double it.
- **Measured:** played on 62% of quiet, 64% of small-hit and 59% of large-hit turns; worth +1.3 ±1.3 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -0.6).

#### Barricade (3 energy; upgraded: costs 2)
Your Block is not removed at the start of your turn.
- **Pays:** nothing this turn. From your next turn, Block left after the enemy turn stays: gain 15 and take 10 a turn, and you start turns with 5, then 10, then 15.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, or one already covered, in a long fight where repeating Block (Crimson Mantle, Stone Armor, Feel No Pain, Rage, Unmovable) outgains the hits.
- **Hold it:** a hit you still need to block: it takes all 3 energy (2 upgraded) and blocks 0 now. A fight ending within about 3 turns (inferred). Block that only just covers each hit leaves nothing to keep.
- **In the turn:** order does not matter. Once it is out, Block from a quiet turn waits for the next hit, so blocking on a quiet turn or past the incoming damage stops being wasted (the exception to general.md 8.3 and 8.4), and Body Slam grows with the pile. Kept Block does not fire Juggernaut again (inferred).
- **Measured:** played on 21% of quiet, 38% of small-hit and 6% of large-hit turns; worth -0.8 ±1.5 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -1.8). Marked (?): mostly left in hand, so this says nothing about a deck built for it.

#### Crimson Mantle (1 energy; upgraded: 10 Block instead of 8)
At the start of your turn, lose 1 HP, then gain 8 flat Block.
- **Pays:** nothing this turn. From the start of your next turn, 8 Block for 1 HP a turn: 32 Block for 4 HP over 4 turns (upgraded 40).
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, as early as you can. On a hit turn it can still beat a Defend: 5 more HP lost now, 7 net back each later enemy turn that hits for 8 or more, so it is ahead after one. Block instead if those 5 HP risk death.
- **Hold it:** the fight ends this turn or next, before an enemy turn its Block would cover.
- **In the turn:** order does not matter. Its HP loss counts as lost on your turn: Rupture +1 Strength, Inferno 6 to ALL, Spite's extra hit, Tear Asunder +1 hit. Juggernaut fires on its Block; Unmovable does not double it.
- **Measured:** played on 92% of quiet, 89% of small-hit and 76% of large-hit turns; worth +11.0 ±1.7 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +8.8). Bosses +16.0, elites +6.1.

#### Juggernaut (2 energy; upgraded: 8 damage instead of 6)
Whenever you gain Block, deal 6 flat damage to a random enemy.
- **Pays:** from the next Block gain, this turn included: 2 gains a turn over 4 turns is 2 x 6 x 4 = 48. Strength and Vulnerable do not raise it; enemy Block absorbs it.
- **Play it:** a turn with 2 or more Block gains to follow, which repay it at once (12 damage, two Strikes' worth), or a quiet turn. At 3 energy, Juggernaut then Defend gives 5 Block and 6 damage; Defend and two Strikes give 5 and 12 (no Strength or Vulnerable). Level after one more gain, ahead after that.
- **Hold it:** a big hit that needs the 2 energy for Block cards. 2 or fewer Block gains left in the fight, this turn's included (upgraded 1 or fewer): 2 gains only match the 12 of two Strikes. Several enemies when one must die first (random target).
- **In the turn:** before every Block card. Each gain fires it once, whatever its size, Unmovable's doubled card too: Rage then 3 Attacks is 18, Second Wind on 3 cards 18, Evil Eye after an exhaust 12. Feel No Pain, Stone Armor and Crimson Mantle fire it on each payment.
- **Measured:** played on 75% of quiet, 73% of small-hit and 48% of large-hit turns; worth +4.5 ±1.7 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +2.7). Bosses +6.1, elites +2.8 (the gap is inside the intervals).

#### Unmovable (2 energy; upgraded: costs 1)
The first card that gives you Block each turn gives double.
- **Pays:** this turn if a Block card follows and none came before, then every turn: a Defend a turn over 4 turns is 4 x 5 = 20 more Block.
- **Play it:** a high-damage turn with one Block card in hand. Unmovable then Defend blocks 10; Defend and two Strikes block 5 and deal 12: take the 10 when 5 HP matter more or the fight is long. Upgraded, then Flame Barrier: 24 Block for 3 energy (Flame Barrier and Defend: 17). A quiet turn costs no HP.
- **Hold it:** a hit you must block and no Block card in hand: it gives 0 now.
- **In the turn:** before any Block card (Iron Wave, Taunt and Armaments use it up too), biggest first: with Unmovable already out (or upgraded, at 1 energy), Shrug It Off then Defend is 16 + 5 = 21, Defend first 10 + 8 = 18. Second Wind and Evil Eye double every gain. Rage, Stone Armor, Feel No Pain and Crimson Mantle are not doubled.
- **Measured:** played on 58% of quiet, 80% of small-hit and 52% of large-hit turns; worth +4.1 ±1.6 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +0.5). Bosses +5.6, elites +2.6 (the gap is inside the intervals). Marked (low): more than the pick-values row says.

#### Tank (1 energy; upgraded: costs 0)
Multiplayer only. You take double attack damage. Other players take half.
- **Pays:** from the coming enemy turn, for the rest of the fight. Its text does not move attacks onto you: 10 aimed at you becomes 20, 10 aimed at a teammate becomes 5.
- **Play it:** when you have the most Block in the party (Crimson Mantle, Stone Armor, Barricade) and more damage is aimed at teammates than at you, on a turn you can block your doubled hits.
- **Hold it:** 2 players, attacks on both and no spare Block: 10 each becomes 20 + 5 = 25 taken instead of 20. With 4 players it saves even then (20 + 15 = 35 against 40).
- **In the turn:** play it before you block, then check whether the intent on you doubled; if not, block for double (not verified which). Tell the party.
- **Measured:** not measured (multiplayer only).

### Engines

#### Inferno (1 energy; upgraded: 9 damage instead of 6)
At the start of your turn, lose 1 HP. Whenever you lose HP on your turn, deal 6 flat damage to ALL.
- **Pays:** at once on HP you lose later this turn. Its own 1 HP loss starts next turn, then buys 6 to each enemy every turn: 24 per enemy over your next 4 turns (72 against 3). Flat: Strength doesn't raise it; enemy Block absorbs it.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, with 1 energy spare after the Block you need, or before a self-damage card this turn.
- **Hold it:** the fight ends this turn (or next, against one enemy) with no self-damage card to follow; a big hit that needs all your energy for Block and nothing triggers it now; very low HP (Block never stops the 1 HP).
- **In the turn:** before Bloodletting, Offering, Brand, Breakthrough, Hemokinesis, Blood Wall. One trigger per loss: Offering's 6 HP deals 6, like Brand's 1. Crimson Mantle's loss triggers it too. Its own loss feeds Rupture and Tear Asunder and turns on Spite's 2 hits.
- **Measured:** played on 87% of quiet, 79% of small-hit and 67% of large-hit turns; worth +6.5 ±1.7 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +4.2). Bosses +7.0, elites +6.0 (no clear difference).

#### Stampede (2 energy; upgraded: costs 1)
When you end your turn, 1 random Attack in your hand is played for free.
- **Pays:** at this turn's end if an Attack is left in hand, then each turn you leave one: a Strike is 6 a turn, 24 over 4 turns; a Bludgeon you can't afford is 32.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, or when an Attack costing at least what Stampede costs would sit in hand anyway (Bludgeon, Mangle, Unrelenting): it pays back this turn.
- **Hold it:** a big hit needs its energy for Block; no Attack would stay in hand; the fight ends this turn.
- **In the turn:** play it, then the rest, leaving your costliest Attack the only one in hand. It hits a random enemy before the enemy turn, so a kill or Mangle's Strength loss counts against the coming hits. It uses up a pending Unrelenting charge; Rage gives Block for it. A Whirlwind left in hand uses your unspent energy (0 left, 0 hits).
- **Measured:** not in the card table (too few turns to play it on).

#### Dark Embrace (2 energy; upgraded: costs 1)
Whenever a card of yours is exhausted, draw 1.
- **Pays:** at once, 1 card per exhaust: 2 a turn over 4 turns is 8 cards. A played Exhaust card counts (Tremble, Offering); a played Power does not.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, or before this turn's exhaust cards. On a big-hit turn, only if you can still play what it draws.
- **Hold it:** nothing to exhaust this turn or soon; the fight ends this turn or next, unless this turn's exhausts draw what you need; a hand near 10 (draws stop there).
- **In the turn:** before the exhaust cards: Burning Pact then draws 3, Brand draws 1. Exhaust before Battle Trance, after which it draws nothing. Each Skill under Corruption draws 1. Cards drawn during Second Wind or Fiend Fire stay in hand: no extra Block or hit.
- **Measured:** played on 22% of quiet, 27% of small-hit and 12% of large-hit turns; worth -2.4 ±1.2 HP over a Strike (one-turn script -2.3). Marked (?): mostly left in hand, so this says nothing about a deck built for it.

#### Hellraiser (2 energy; upgraded: costs 1)
Whenever you draw a Strike-tagged card (Strike, Perfected, Pommel, Twin, Setup, Ashen), it is played at once for free on a random enemy.
- **Pays:** on your next draw: this turn if a draw card follows it, otherwise next turn's hand. With 5 Strike-tagged cards in 15, a 5-card hand holds about 1.7: about 1.7 energy a turn for your other cards (7 over 4 turns), worth it when you have more cards than energy.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, or before this turn's draw cards (Pommel Strike, Shrug It Off, Burning Pact, Offering, Battle Trance).
- **Hold it:** a big hit and no draw after it; the fight ends this turn or next.
- **In the turn:** draw cards after it. Free Strikes are not replaced, so the hand is smaller. Rage before the draws gives 3 Block per free Strike. A free Pommel Strike's draw can trigger it again (inferred). Turn-start Strikes hit a random enemy before this turn's Vulnerable.
- **Measured:** played on 69% of quiet, 76% of small-hit and 53% of large-hit turns; worth +2.7 ±1.6 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +1.7).

#### Pyre (2 energy; upgraded: 2 more energy each turn instead of 1)
You get 1 more energy each turn, from next turn.
- **Pays:** nothing this turn, then +1 energy every turn: even after 2 turns, net +2 over your next 4 turns. Upgraded: even after 1 turn, net +6 over 4.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn in a long fight (the turns listed under Demon Form). Bloodletting (3 HP for 2 energy) can pay for it.
- **Hold it:** the fight ends within 2 more turns (1 upgraded); a big hit (2 of 3 energy, nothing back this turn); a hand you already play out every turn.
- **In the turn:** draw and 0-cost cards first, then Pyre and your best 1-cost card. Later turns, spend the extra on 3-cost and X cards (Bludgeon, Demon Form, Mangle, Whirlwind, Cascade) or draw.
- **Measured:** played on 71% of quiet, 61% of small-hit and 40% of large-hit turns; worth +3.0 ±1.5 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +1.5).

#### Corruption (3 energy; upgraded: costs 2)
Your Skills cost 0 and are exhausted when played.
- **Pays:** at once: each Skill after it saves its cost (2 for Impervious, Flame Barrier) and is gone for the fight, Defends too. Skills with Exhaust (Impervious, Offering, Tremble) lose nothing. Cascade still spends all your energy.
- **Play it:** a quiet turn, then play no Defend you don't need; or a big hit with 4 Skills in hand: Corruption, Defend, Defend, Shrug It Off, Taunt is 25 Block (upgraded, 1 energy left) against 20 at most without.
- **Hold it:** few Skills; a long fight where your Block is mostly Defends and no Feel No Pain or Dark Embrace replaces them; Test Subject phase 1 (it gains Strength per Skill you play; unverified): Corruption pays only through Skills, so its stun turn suits it better.
- **In the turn:** Feel No Pain and Dark Embrace before the Skills (3 Block and 1 draw per Skill). After one Skill, Evil Eye gives 8 Block twice and Forgotten Ritual 3 energy. Drum of Battle draws 2 and gives 2 energy.
- **Measured:** played on 55% of quiet, 59% of small-hit and 41% of large-hit turns; worth +2.9 ±1.6 HP over a Strike (one-turn script +0.8).
