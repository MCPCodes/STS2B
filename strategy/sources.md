## LLM-plays-STS2
- https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02255 and https://arxiv.org/html/2607.02255 (H): the AgenticSTS paper.
  - Silent at A0, v0.103.1/0.103.3. A no-memory baseline won 3/10; adding triggered strategic skills won 6/10 (p≈0.37, directional).

## Confidence notes: outdated or contradictory items
1. **Version drift:**
   - The caller expects v0.103.x. Main moved to v0.107.1 (Jun 19) and beta to v0.111 (Aug).
   - Changed after v0.103.x: Doormaker → Aeonglass, Shatter nerf, Fusion and Synthesis changes, Predator to Common, Follow Through → Scare, Debilitate nerf, Drum of Battle and Conflagration reworks, Diamond Diadem rework, Skulking Colony buffs, Regalite and Alignment changes, Blade of Ink nerfs.
   - Trust in-game text.
2. **Borrowed Time:** official v0.103.2 notes say 1 cost, +4 (6) Energy, cards cost +1 this turn. sts2.gg says v0.103 reverted it to 0 cost with self-Doom. Conflict, so read the card.
3. **Regent and Necrobinder starter relic names:** Divine Right and Bound Phylactery (wiki and gamewith, v0.107.1) vs Crown of Stars and Osty's Binding (older Mobalytics and sts2.gg). Probably renamed.
4. **A2:** "Ancients heal only 80% of missing HP" (untapped, PCGamesN) vs "max HP -20%" (BrokenBuilds) vs a fully different list (metabot.gg, ignored). I used the first.
5. **A6:** Gloom (fewer rest sites) before v0.103.2; Inflation (removal 100, +50) after. PCGamesN still lists Gloom.
6. **Act 1 elite count:** untapped says 2-3, sts2.gg says 1-2. Guide says 1-2, more if strong.
7. **Skulking Colony:** sts2.gg advises "burst"; untapped data shows a per-turn HP-loss cap. I followed the cap logic.
8. **Co-op enemy targeting:** "attacks hit every player" (Wikipedia, Mobalytics, BrokenBuilds) vs per-ally redirect cards (Intercept) implying aimed attacks. Unresolved.
9. **Co-op map:** vote plus weighted RNG (allthings.how) vs no vote (pre-release slaythespire2.gg) vs agree-or-RNG (BrokenBuilds).
10. **Co-op events:** per-player outcomes vs group vote. Probably both exist by event.
11. **Co-op revive:** 1 HP after combat (most sources) vs a rest-site revive costing max HP (dtgre, unverified).
12. **Archaic Tooth mappings** (Bash→Break, Neutralize→Suppress, Unleash→Protector, Falling Star→Meteor Shower...) are inferred from Ancient card names, not confirmed.
13. **Boss relic drops:** unclear whether act bosses drop relics in STS2 (Lava Rock text implies the Act 1 boss drops one).
14. **Win-rate aggregate:** a third-party sample of about 23k runs at v0.107.1. The spread is small. Treat the order, not the numbers, as meaningful.
15. **Infested Prism "Vital Spark":** wording unclear (energy to the attacker?). Reworked in v0.106.
